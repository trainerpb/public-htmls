Core Terminologies

CO (Course Outcomes)

Specific, measurable statements defining exactly what a student should know, understand, or be able to do at the end of a specific course. Mapped using Bloom's Taxonomy.

PO (Program Outcomes)

Macro-level graduate attributes defined by the NBA. These are 12 standard skills that students should possess by the time they graduate from the engineering program.

PSO (Program Specific Outcomes)

Statements describing what the graduates of a specific engineering discipline (like CSE) should be able to do.

PEO (Program Educational Objectives)

Broad statements describing the career and professional accomplishments that the program is preparing graduates to achieve 3 to 5 years after graduation.
